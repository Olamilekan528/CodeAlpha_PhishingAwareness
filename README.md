# Phishing Awareness Training

## What it is
This is a training presentation about phishing, written for people without a technical background. It’s Task 2 of the CodeAlpha Cyber Security internship, with 13 slides and a 6-question quiz.

## What it covers
It explains what phishing is and why it matters, then shows how to spot phishing emails and fake websites. It also goes through the types of phishing (email, spear phishing, smishing, vishing, business email compromise), social engineering tactics, real-world examples, best practices, and what to do if you clicked.

## Files in this repo

## What I learned
I was surprised that a padlock only means the connection is encrypted, so a fake site can have one too. I also learned that a message with no spelling mistakes isn’t automatically safe, and that phone and SMS attacks work better than email, so I shouldn’t relax just because it isn’t an email.
